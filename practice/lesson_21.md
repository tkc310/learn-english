# Lesson21

## Lesson20の復習

### 1. 意味を変える

1. Misaki is pretty. (わたしよりも)<br>
Misaki is prettier than me.
2. Yuko studied English hard. (3人の中で)<br>
Yuko studied English the hardest of the three.


### 2. 正しい単語を選ぶ

1. This song is the most famous (in) Brazil.
2. She drives (the most) carefully of the five.
3. That man looked saddest (in) all.
4. His manner was the (worst) in his family.


### 3. 単語を並び替える

1. (more / that one / runs / than / this car / slowly / .)<br>
This car runs more slowly than that one.
2. It was (of / wonderful / all / most / them / movie / the / .)<br>
It was the most wonderful movie of all them.
3. She (popular / us / was / most / all / the / of / student / .)<br>
She was the most popular student of us all.


### 4. 英文を書き換える

1. This movie is interesting. (this one を文末に加えて比較級)<br>
This movie is more interesting than this one.
2. He always finishes his work quickly. (in his company を文末に加えて最上級)<br>
He always finishes his work the most quickly in his company.


## Lesson21の演習

### 1. ほぼ同じ意味の英文を作る

1. It is warmer today than yesterday.<br>
It was [cooler] yesterday [than] today.
2. This pen is more useful than that one.<br>
That pen [isn't] more [useful] [than] this one.
3. Your car looks newer than mine.<br>
My car looks [older] [than] [yours].
4. He is 30 years old and his grandmother 90 years old.<br>
His grandmother is [three] [time] as old [as] he.
5. The boy can swim faster than you.<br>
You [can't] swim [as] [fast] [as] the boy.
6. His pocket is wider than yours.<br>
Your pocket isn't [as] [wide] [as] [his].


### 2. 適切な単語を入れる

1. この曲とあの曲ではどちらが好きだった？<br>
[Which] did you like [better], this song [or] that one?
2. 僕は夕あなたほどよく寝れなかった<br>
I [couldn't] sleep [as] well [as] you last night.
3. 彼女は日本語より英語のほうが好きだ<br>
She likes English [better] [than] Japanese.
4. 私の車はあなたのよりずっと古く見える<br>
My car [looks] [much] [older] than yours.
5. 彼は他のどの選手より一生懸命練習した<br>
He practiced [harder] [than] [any] [other] player.
