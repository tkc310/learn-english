# Lesson19

## Lesson18の復習

### 1. 誤った英文を書き直す

1. (a) What time did you finish washing the windows?
2. (d) We hope to have dinner with Mr. Mizushima some day.


### 2. 単語を並び替える (不要な単語がある)

1. He ( forward / dance / with / looking / is / dancing / to ) you.<br>
He is looking forward to dance with you. (彼はあなたと踊ることを楽しみにしている) -> looking forward は「楽しみにしている」
2. She ( without / to say / decided / move / saying / to ) anything.<br>
She decided to move whithout saying anything. (彼女は何も言わず引っ越すことを決心した) -> without -ing で「〜せずに」

> [!NOTE]
> 肯定文での anything は「何でも、どんなものでも」の意味になる


### 3. 単語の役割を選ぶ

ア: 主語 / イ: 目的語 / ウ: 補語 / エ: 修飾語

1. The old man stopped (to wait) for his wife. (エ) -> wait for で「〜を待つ」
2. (Speaking) English is a lot of fun. (ア)
3. Her job is (writing) an article in a monthly magazine. (ウ)
4. We love (talking) with you. (イ) -> talk with で「〜と話す」
5. She is good at (drawing) pictures. (イ) -> drawing が at(前置詞) の目的語になっている
6. To (go) there can be very dangerous. (ア)


## Lesson19の演習

### 1. 適切な接続詞を選ぶ

when / or / if / because / that / and / but

1. He was very rich (but) he wasn't happy.
2. Which do you like, rice (or) bread?
3. She is cute (and) her mother is kind.
4. I was sleeping (when) my mother called me.
5. You don't know (that) the amusement park is far from the center of Kobe. -> far from で「〜から遠い」
6. (Because) you didn't study hard, you couldn't pass the examination.

> [!NOTE]
> examination: 試験
> exam: 試験のフランクな言い方


### 2. thatが省略できる英文を選ぶ

ア. That woman looks very happy.
イ. I hear that he left Osaka for Tokyo last Sunday.
ウ. Is that your card?
エ. Hey, don't do that.

-> イ


### 3. 適切な単語を入れる

1. それは面白そうだけど、私は家に一日中いなければならない<br>
`I [ must ] stay home all day long [ though ] it sounds interesting.` -> all day long で「一日中」
2. もし今夜、彼女が私に電話してきたら「ありがとう」と言う<br>
`I'll say "Thank you" [ if ] she [ calls ] [ me ] tonight.` -> 副詞は条件を表すため現在形 (calls)
3. 私は申し訳ないと思ったので彼女にメールを送った<br>
`I felt sorry [ so ] I sent an email to her.`
4. 彼が帰ってきたというのは本当です<br>
`It is true [ that ] he came back.`
5. その少年はその箱を盗んだが私の息子は盗まなかった<br>
`The boy stole the box [ but ] my son [ didn't ].`
6. 彼がカナダに行く予定だと聞き私はとても悲しい<br>
`I'm so sad to hear he [ will ] [ go ] [ to ] [ Canada ].`


> [!NOTE]
> 「理由 + so + 結論」は「結論 + because + 理由」の反対
> - so: 結論を強調したいとき
> - because: 理由を強調したいとき
