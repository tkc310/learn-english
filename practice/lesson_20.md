# Lesson20

## Lesson19の復習 

### 1. 正しい英文に直す

1. When the shop will open, I'll go shopping at once. -> at once で「すぐに」<br>
When the shop opens, I'll go shopping at once. -> 前半は副詞節のため現在形
2. We didn't know that our daughter wants to work.<br>
We didn't know that our daughter wanted to work. -> 主節が過去形のため従属節も過去形


### 2. 英文の続きを選択

a. because he wanted to marry me.<br>
b. he can't go there.<br>
c. though I didn't want to.<br>
d. so he got angry at me.<br>

1. I was very lazy ... -> d
2. I had to stay there ... -> c
3. We know ... -> b
4. He worked very hard to earn money ... -> a


### 3. 単語を並び替える

1. Ken (you/when/her/or/have to/help) I'm at work.<br>
Ken or you have to help her when I'm at work.
2. You (to/next/he's/leave/know/Kobe/going) Monday.<br>
You know he's going to leave Kobe next Monday.


### 4. 正しい文法上の名称を選ぶ

ア. 副詞句<br>
イ. 副詞節<br>
ウ. 名詞句<br>
エ. 名詞節<br>

1. I don't think (my father is angry). -> エ
2. That is a (very hard question). -> ウ
3. You lived in Okinawa (when I was a student). -> イ


## Lesson20の演習

### 1. 比較級 / 最上級に変換

1. useful - 比較級: more useful / 最上級: the most useful
2. little - 比較級: less / 最上級: the least
3. early - 比較級: more earlier / 最上級: the most earliest
4. large - 比較級: larger / 最上級: the largest
5. goot - 比較級: better / 最上級: best


### 2. 正しい語句を選ぶ

1. Mayu is (more) beautiful than that woman.
2. Ayako is (the) cutest (in) the class.
3. His business was (the most) important of all.
4. He had to study (the hardest) of them all.


### 3. 単語を適切な形にする

1. This area is the (hottest) in Wakayama.
2. Your cup isn't (larger) than mine.
3. The (old) radio is my grandfather's. -> grandfather's のsは所有格なので比較でない
4. Did she get up the (earliest) of us?


### 4. 正しい英文を書く

1. これは神戸で一番大きい店？<br>
Is this [the] [biggest] [shop] in Kobe?
2. アンナは彼女よりも美しくなるだろう<br>
Anna will be [more] beautiful [than] [she].
3. 彼女は私たちみんなの中で１番熱心に勉強した<br>
She studied [hardest] [of] us [all].
4. 今回はあなたが私より早くここに来ることができる？<br>
Can you here [earlier] [than] I this time? -> this time で「今回は」
5. その女の子は５人の中で１番可愛かった<br>
The girl was [the] [cutest] [of] [the] five. -> five は不特定ではないため the が必要
