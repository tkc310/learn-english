# Lesson23

## Lesson22の復習

### 1. 指定の語句を主語にした受け身表現にする

1. The man tells me (the story).<br>
The story is told to me by the man. -> 間接目的語の人(me)には前置詞がつく
2 Satoshi and Azusa named (the baby) Eiji.<br>
The baby was named Eiji by Satoshi and Azusa.


### 2. 単語を並び替える

1. (spoken / Canada / French / in / is / .)<br>
French is spoken in Canada.
2. (by / her child / then / was / the statue / crying /.)<br>
Her child was crying by The statue then. (彼女が子供のその時、銅像の近くで泣いていた)

> [!NOTE]
> 過去進行形のため動詞は crying になり、by は「〜によって」ではなく「〜のそばで」という意味の前置詞になる。


### 3. 受け身表現の英文を能動態にする

Was this tower build by the emperor?<br>
Did the emperor build this tower?


### 4. ほぼ同じ内容になるように単語を入れる

1. What language do they speak in Hong Kong?<br>
What language [is] [spoken] [in] Hong Kong?
2. A strong earthquake killed many people.<br>
Many people [were] [killed] [in] a strong earthquake. (多くの人々が強い地震によってなくなった)
3. How old is your house?<br>
[When] [was] your house [build]?


## Lesson23の演習

### 1. 受け身を能動態に、能動態を受け身にする

1. This room can be used by the students.<br>
The students can use this room.
2. We has to finish this job.<br>
This job is had to be finished by us. -> 不定詞の受け身は「to + be + 過去分詞」


### 2. 和訳する

1. The people were interested in knowing about Japan.<br>
その人々は日本について知ることに興味があった
2. I was too busy to eat after I arrived in Osaka.<br>
私は大阪についた後にあまりにも忙しくて食事ができなかった
3. He plays not only soccer but also baseball.<br>
彼はサッカーだけでなく野球もできる
